Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-08-21

Domestique is free software under the AGPL-3.0 — anyone can run their own copy. This page describes the instance operated at app.domestique.dev, not every possible deployment.

What's collected

  • Account information — your email address and name, handled by our authentication provider (Auth0) when you sign in with a password or with Google.
  • Route data — the GPX files and routes you upload or import, and the crews you share them with.
  • Head unit connections — if you link a Garmin, Wahoo, or Komoot account, the OAuth credentials needed to sync your own routes to your own device. These are encrypted at rest and used only to talk to that provider on your behalf.
  • Basic operational logs — the same kind any web server keeps (request timing, errors) for keeping the service running, not for tracking you.

What's not collected

No advertising, no analytics or tracking scripts, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties beyond the services named below — each contacted only to do the specific job it's there for.

Who else sees it

  • Auth0 — authentication (sign-in, password reset, MFA).
  • SMTP2GO — delivering account emails (invites, password resets).
  • Garmin, Wahoo, and Komoot — only for accounts you explicitly link, and only to read or write your own routes.

Your data

Deleting your account removes your linked head unit credentials. Routes you've shared with a crew are that crew's own call to remove. Since this is self-hosted, open-source software, you're also welcome to just ask — see the contact below.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@domestique.dev.
